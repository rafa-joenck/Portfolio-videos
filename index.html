How the videos were made

The work starts from reference documents in Word or PDF, converted to Markdown and organized in an AI-supported workflow to help create instructional training videos for Benner systems.

AI is one stage of the process. What it generates goes through my work on three fronts:

Data curation

Evaluating and selecting the data the AI generated from the documentation.

Caption and context review

Reviewing the captions and context of the content produced.

Screen capture

Recording the screen footage that shows the systems in use in the videos.
